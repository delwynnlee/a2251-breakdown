## Asset availability

- **Tier 1:** no verified, redistributable A2251 CAD obtained. No proprietary schematic or asset redistributed.
- **Tier 2:** exterior plates use Apple's published width/depth. Corner radii, thickness distribution, keyboard openings, port cutouts and hinge mechanics remain estimates. This is not manufacturing CAD.
- **Tier 3:** all internals and small hardware are procedural teaching proxies. Package blocks do not assert real board layout; no part numbers or chip identities are inferred from appearance. Fan blades, screws, and key positions are representative.
- No manufacturer's datasheet is attached to an unidentified package. Matching a datasheet requires an independently verified package marking and board revision first.

## Evidence rules

`verified` means a specific stated specification or component existence is supported by the linked source. It never means the entire mesh is verified. `reference-pending` means a plausible model-specific reference exists but does not verify the particular claim. `illustrative` means a teaching proxy. Scene position and dimensions are separate from specification confidence.

Thermal and power lines are conceptual pathways, not measured heat maps or reconstructed schematics. Data edges show functional relationships and explicitly allow omitted intermediary controllers. The disassembly tab summarizes a sourced sequence but does not simulate each tool motion, adhesive release or connector latch.

All source-backed and approximate entries are in [the inventory](hardware-inventory.md), with a machine-readable [manifest](component-manifest.json).
